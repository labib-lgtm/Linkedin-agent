### Patterns (every winner does ≥4 of 5)
- **P1** — Currency in line 1 or 2 ($/€/£)
- **P2** — Two-line contradiction hook (setup → contradiction)
- **P3** — Story arc, not a Step 1/2/3 framework
- **P4** — Two concrete before→after numbers (CVR, ACoS, CPC)
- **P5** — One-word lead magnet keyword + DM funnel

### Bonus pattern
- **P6** — Internal vocabulary (naming the pattern: "internal cannibalization", "match type bleed") drives quote-back comments and re-shares

### Anti-patterns (losers do these)
- Generic "Most sellers..." opener with no $
- Multi-step "Step 1, Step 2..." framework
- Hot take with no story payoff
- Tool name dropped in line 4 (reads like an ad)
